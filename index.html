About

I am a research assistant with a doctorate in Computer Science (Dr.-Ing., magna cum laude) specialised in real-time, safety-critical embedded systems. I am part of the AEMY Lab (sAfe sEcure and sMart sYstems) at Hochschule München University of Applied Sciences, where my research focuses on formal verification of C++ with large language models and agentic coding assistants, as well as building fully local, confidential AI tooling for sensitive codebases. From time-triggered organic computing for autonomous driving to deep-learning accelerators, the goal is the same: dependable systems that behave correctly in the real world.
